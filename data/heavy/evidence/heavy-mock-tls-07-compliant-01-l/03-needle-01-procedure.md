# Internal procedure — Modern TLS for public web services

Fictional reference: mock-tls-07-compliant-01. This sample is mock evidence for a student benchmark.

## Purpose
This procedure describes the local operating expectation for service owners and the platform team.

## Requirement
All public endpoints must accept TLS 1.2 or newer; older protocol versions are disabled in the shared proxy profile.

## Roles and records
The service owner maintains the evidence. Operations records exceptions and tracks assigned work through closure.
