# Internal procedure — Password strength for workforce accounts

Fictional reference: mock-pwd-01-non-compliant-01. This sample is mock evidence for a student benchmark.

## Purpose
This procedure describes the local operating expectation for service owners and the platform team.

## Requirement
Passwords should be long and difficult to guess. Exceptions may be approved by a manager.

## Roles and records
The service owner maintains the evidence. Operations records exceptions and tracks assigned work through closure.
