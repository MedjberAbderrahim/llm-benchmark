# Internal procedure — Repeated sign-in failure handling

Fictional reference: mock-lock-03-compliant-01. This sample is mock evidence for a student benchmark.

## Purpose
This procedure describes the local operating expectation for service owners and the platform team.

## Requirement
After five consecutive failures, the account is suspended for 20 minutes. The service desk can verify identity before an early reset.

## Roles and records
The service owner maintains the evidence. Operations records exceptions and tracks assigned work through closure.
