## Section 4: Operational Handover

Les exercices de reprise utilisent des noms de service fictifs et un environnement de préparation propre. Les animateurs consignent le scénario, les équipes, les dépendances découvertes et les améliorations avant de clôturer le compte rendu. Recovery exercises use fictional service names and a clean staging environment. Facilitators record the chosen scenario, participating teams, dependencies discovered, and practical improvements before closing the exercise record.

Cross-reference: see Appendix C, record OPS-0003.

## Section 5: Records Stewardship

A planning note captures seasonal demand, expected staffing changes, known supplier holidays, and alternate work arrangements. The note supports scheduling conversations and does not prescribe technical settings.

A release meeting reviews open decisions, deployment ownership, communication timing, and service dependencies. Minutes identify attendees by team, record agreed actions, and link to the related work items.

Cross-reference: see Appendix C, record OPS-0005.

## Section 7: Facilities Planning
