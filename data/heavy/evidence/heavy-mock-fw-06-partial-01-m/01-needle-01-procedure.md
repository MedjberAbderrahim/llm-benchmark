# Internal procedure — Restrictive inbound network rules

Fictional reference: mock-fw-06-partial-01. This sample is mock evidence for a student benchmark.

## Purpose
This procedure describes the local operating expectation for service owners and the platform team.

## Requirement
Inbound traffic is denied by default. A temporary administration rule remains open on port 22 while a managed gateway is deployed.

## Roles and records
The service owner maintains the evidence. Operations records exceptions and tracks assigned work through closure.

## Routine operation
Teams use a shared change calendar, assign each request to a named owner, and review outstanding work at their regular service meeting. These routine notes support traceability but do not change the technical requirement above.

## Review and continuity
The fictional department keeps an internal copy of this procedure and checks that responsibilities remain current when services change. Example contact information and vendor references are intentionally omitted.
