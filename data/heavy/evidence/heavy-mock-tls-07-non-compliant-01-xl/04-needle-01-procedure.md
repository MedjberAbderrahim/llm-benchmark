## Purpose
This procedure describes the local operating expectation for service owners and the platform team.

## Requirement
The approved profile says TLS 1.2 is preferred. This active listener still enables TLS 1.0 for legacy clients.

## Roles and records
The service owner maintains the evidence. Operations records exceptions and tracks assigned work through closure.

## Routine operation
Teams use a shared change calendar, assign each request to a named owner, and review outstanding work at their regular service meeting. These routine notes support traceability but do not change the technical requirement above.
