# Internal procedure — Security event retention and availability

Fictional reference: mock-log-04-partial-01. This sample is mock evidence for a student benchmark.

## Purpose
This procedure describes the local operating expectation for service owners and the platform team.

## Requirement
The platform retains searchable logs for 90 days. Archived copies are kept for a further 180 days.

## Roles and records
The service owner maintains the evidence. Operations records exceptions and tracks assigned work through closure.
