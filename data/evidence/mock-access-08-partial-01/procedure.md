# Internal procedure — Periodic review of user access

Fictional reference: mock-access-08-partial-01. This sample is mock evidence for a student benchmark.

## Purpose
This procedure describes the local operating expectation for service owners and the platform team.

## Requirement
Les responsables vérifient les comptes deux fois par an. Les suppressions sont transmises au support, sans confirmation systématique.

## Roles and records
The service owner maintains the evidence. Operations records exceptions and tracks assigned work through closure.
