# Internal procedure — Restrictive inbound network rules

Fictional reference: mock-fw-06-compliant-01. This sample is mock evidence for a student benchmark.

## Purpose
This procedure describes the local operating expectation for service owners and the platform team.

## Requirement
Inbound connections are denied unless a service owner approves a named source and destination port. The perimeter uses a default-deny policy.

## Roles and records
The service owner maintains the evidence. Operations records exceptions and tracks assigned work through closure.
