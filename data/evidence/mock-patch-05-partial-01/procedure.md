# Internal procedure — Timely critical security updates

Fictional reference: mock-patch-05-partial-01. This sample is mock evidence for a student benchmark.

## Purpose
This procedure describes the local operating expectation for service owners and the platform team.

## Requirement
Critical updates should be installed within 45 days when operations allow. Exceptions are discussed at the monthly meeting.

## Roles and records
The service owner maintains the evidence. Operations records exceptions and tracks assigned work through closure.
